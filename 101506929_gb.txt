George Brown Polytechnic is a public post-secundary institution located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It is an institution 
that combine leaning paths with hand-on practices to prepare students for the challenges of the working workd of today and tomorrow.

The institution offers diploma, degree, and postgraduate programs in areas such as technology, business, health sciences, and community services. George Brown Polytechnic focuses on applied education, 
allowing students to work with real industry tools and complete projects that reflect workplace environments.
Graduates are well prepared for the workforce because of the institutions focus on experiential learning and modern technical skills.

The polytechnic has multiple campuses, including St. James, Casa Loma, and Waterfront, all located in the downtown of Toronto.

The future of George Brown College presents a range of exciting possibilities as we prepare students for a world of 
constant change—as economic transformations, climate change, urbanization and disruptive technologies continue to shake up established industries.
