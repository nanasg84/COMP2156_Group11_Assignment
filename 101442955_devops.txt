<h1>Overview of GBC COMP 2156 DevOps Course</h1>

<p>
The COMP 2156 course at George Brown College focuses on the DevOps lifecycle, emphasizing the 
collaboration between software developers and operations teams. The course covers essential 
DevOps principles, tools, and practices, including version control, CI, and containerization. 
Students will learn to build CI/CD pipelines using tools like Jenkins and Maven, and containerize 
apps using Docker and Kubernetes. 

The course also includes hands-on tutorials on setting up repositories, 
managing branches, and collaborating within a development team. By the end of the course, students 
will be proficient in setting up CI/CD pipelines, managing infrastructure as code with Ansible, 
and monitoring applications with Prometheus and Grafana. This course is designed to equip students 
with practical skills and knowledge to excel in the dynamic field of DevOps.
</p>
