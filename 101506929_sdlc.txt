DevOps is really important in the software development cycle because it improves collaboration between development and operations teams. 
Instead of working separately, both teams work together throughout the entire process, from planning to deployment and maintenance. 
Continuos Integrations and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) allow to automated pipelines tests and deploy the aplication quicky, 
helping the fast develop of the application. 
DevOps aldo improve the process of monitoring and feedback because the use of tools that track applications in real time allow the teams to fix issues quickly.
DevOps makes the SDLC faster, more efficient, and more collaborative.
