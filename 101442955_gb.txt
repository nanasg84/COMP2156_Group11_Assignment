About George Brown College

George Brown College was established in 1967 and is located in downtown Toronto, Ontario. It offers over 170 full-time programs, including diplomas, 
degrees, and postgraduate certificates across various disciplines such as business, health sciences, community services, culinary arts, design, and technology. 
The college is known for its strong emphasis on applied learning, providing students with opportunities to gain real-world experience through 
co-op placements, internships, and collaborative projects with industry partners.
